A SMALL PAINTING GUIDE

Think beyond the hex.

Use the studio as a starting point, then trust your eye.

01

Shift hue, not just value.

Warm light often pairs beautifully with cooler shadows. Cool lighting reverses that relationship. Pigment shifts hue, saturation, and lightness together to give you a more expressive starting point.

02

Let harmonies do the heavy lifting.

Analogous colors create cohesion. Complementary colors create tension. Triadic palettes spread color around the wheel, while split-complementary palettes soften the contrast.

03

Check the values.

Turn on Grayscale view to see whether light and dark areas still read clearly. HSL lightness is not perceived luminance, so two different hues with the same lightness may look very different in grayscale.

04

Keep your layers flexible.

Multiply darkens underlying colors; Add (Glow) builds light; Overlay increases contrast. Names and results vary by painting app. Start with the suggested opacity, then adjust for your actual canvas.
